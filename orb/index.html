My Process

  1. 1

    Define the problem and who it affects most

    Before drawing screens, we agree on who the product is for, what resource limitations we have, and how we’ll measure success. Early alignment saves weeks of polishing the wrong thing.

  2. 2

    Prototype & test early

    I build clickable prototypes before any designs go into production, so ideas meet real people while they are still cheap to change.

  3. 3

    Design the system

    Then comes the final polish. If we’re building a design system from scratch, I finalize the components and styles and organize them into one resource your team can always come back to.

  4. 4

    Hand off and launch

    I work alongside engineering until release, reviewing builds, answering edge cases and tightening the details that make a product feel finished.

  5. 5

    Measure and refine

    After release, we check the numbers we agreed on at the start, talk to users again and audit what shipped. We agree on a roadmap and get back to building.

And then repeat
